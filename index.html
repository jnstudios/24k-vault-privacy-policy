Privacy Policy for 24K Vault

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

24K Vault ("the App") is designed to help users securely manage and track gold, silver, jewelry, loans, savings goals, and related asset information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected.

Information You Provide

The App may store information entered by you, including:

  • Gold and silver item details
  • Jewelry records
  • Loan information
  • Savings goals
  • Owner and family member details
  • Photos attached to records
  • Application preferences and settings

This information is primarily stored locally on your device.

Photos and Media Access

The App may request permission to access your device's camera or photo library to allow you to attach images to your records.

Photos are used solely for functionality provided by the App and are not sold or shared by the developer.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App may use third-party services including:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics

These services may collect information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic information

Advertising

The App may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process certain device identifiers and usage information to provide and improve advertising services.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by Google services used for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising functionality.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

24K Vault is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may remove your information at any time by deleting records within the App or uninstalling the App from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
